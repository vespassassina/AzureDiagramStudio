## Context And Superseding PO Decision

PO request: "allow dragging connector ends to attach them to a specific point
or reattach to another point.

allow deleting connector ends with Canc key"

The PO initially selected detaching one end, then explicitly superseded it:
"when you press CANC delete the connector completely".

The current requirement is whole-connector deletion. Do not implement the
earlier detached/free-end deletion choice or introduce dangling-end state.
The PO selected "Any point along the border, fixed relative to the item
(Recommended)" for exact connected attachment positions.

## Confirmed Interaction Contract

1. Both connector ends can be dragged to a chosen point along the border of an
   eligible connectable item, including another point on the same item or a
   different item. No restriction to the four midpoint ports.
2. The attachment remains at that relative border position as its item moves,
   resizes, changes parent or participates in layout. Preserve valid endpoint
   identity and explicit approach side. Interior points are not an attachment.
3. Press Canc/Delete on a selected connector or its selected endpoint removes
   the entire connector, not one end or just an arrowhead. Leave connected
   boxes untouched. One Undo restores the whole connector and accepted geometry.
4. Text/Markdown/property inputs keep normal character deletion and IME behavior;
   never interpret Delete there as diagram deletion. Retain ownership,
   Present/modal/typing guards and current multi-selection safety.
5. If Delete occurs during endpoint drag, cancel the preview and remove the
   original connector as one operation; late pointer release cannot recreate it
   or commit another history step. Invalid/empty-space drop or Escape/cancel
   restores the previous connected endpoint, not a free/dangling connector.
6. Exact attachment edits commit once per accepted gesture, no-op/cancel zero
   history, with Undo/Redo, snapping/zoom, keyboard alternatives and real
   visible hit targets. No segment/endpoint/label-edit hit-layer conflicts.
7. Shared render/Present/SVG/PNG/PDF/Visio and page/workbook save/CLI/MCP must
   retain accepted attachments; manual segment reset is not attachment reset.
   Update guide with drag-to-attach/reattach and whole-connector Canc/Delete.

## Approved Persisted Design

The PO approved [RFD005](../rfd/005-exact-anchors-and-grid-style.md):
Diagram v2 optional normalized sourceOffset/targetOffset with explicit sides,
unchanged v1 unless explicitly opting into new features, and mixed supported
page versions inside workbook v1. The historical design rationale below must
not be read as a still-pending approval. Do not add fields beyond that contract.

Current connections persist entity ids, discrete sourceSide/targetSide and
revision-bound route points. Route points reset on geometry changes, so they
cannot silently double as persistent normalized attachment metadata.
Do not use hidden dummy nodes, mutate side enums or overload routeRevision.

Implement the approved backward-compatible persisted exact-border anchor
contract with explicit validation and older-app/version behavior. Further
public fields outside RFD005 still require PO approval. Existing files must
remain readable and must not be automatically migrated/rewritten. Coordinate
with the explicitly amended workbook page versions and headless contracts.

No persisted free-end representation is required: that earlier proposal was
superseded before implementation.

## Given/When/Then Verification

Retain executed behavioral RED/GREEN/typecheck/build and hashes under
`app/test-results/dgm155`, with independent production-browser and output checks.

- Given a selected edge, drag each end to a nonmidpoint border location on the
  same/different box: exact accepted attachment and one history edit.
- Given accepted nonmidpoint attachments, move/resize/reparent/layout their
  boxes, save/reload and export: relative positions remain and real rendered
  endpoints agree with saved data across page/workbook/automation surfaces.
- Given invalid/empty-space drop, Escape, pointer cancel or stale ownership,
  preserve both original connected endpoints and history.
- Given a selected endpoint/connector, press Canc/Delete: whole connection
  disappears and both boxes remain; Undo/Redo restores/removes exact state.
- Given active endpoint drag plus Delete then pointerup, remove once and never
  resurrect the edge. Given active typing/IME/modal/Present, no forbidden delete.
- Given reset manual segments or changing unrelated label/style/background,
  exact attachment persists unless explicitly reconnected/reset by its own
  approved operation.

## Boundaries

No dangling endpoints, arrowhead-only Delete, cross-page connectors, dependency,
security weakening, unrelated DGM-148 segment repair, installed-profile mutation,
package/version/release/install or Git delivery. Native/schema/CLI/MCP changes
are limited to the approved detailed contract above.

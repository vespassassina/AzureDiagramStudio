## Context

PO request: "also when clicking an item do not immediately open the rename
tool or the side bar. if the side bar is open populate it. if i select an item
and THEN click on the title open the rename tool"

Existing selectEntity and edge selection call openInspector(false), which may
open desktop Properties and switch the sidebar view. Whole-node/whole-edge
double-click currently opens inline editing irrespective of label hit.
Replace these automatic behaviors with explicit select-then-title intent.

## Contract And Acceptance Criteria

1. Clicking an unselected canvas item selects it only. Even when that first
   click hits its title/label, do not start inline rename or open a closed
   sidebar. Selection itself changes no document/history or persisted content.
2. A later unmodified click on the title/label of the sole already-selected
   item opens existing inline label editing. A click on its body/icon/line,
   resize edge/handle, connector endpoint or segment handle does not rename.
   Resolve eligibility from selection at pointer admission, before React Flow
   select callbacks can make the same first click look like a subsequent click.
3. This applies consistently to component/boundary/rectangle/text titles and
   connector labels. Empty/unpainted labels keep F2/context-menu alternatives;
   do not invent a hidden canvas target. Whole-body double-click is not an
   alternate immediate rename path. A first click selects, a later title click
   edits; no double-click timing requirement.
4. Modified multi-selection clicks and drags never start inline rename. A
   title drag remains a move; boundary border drag belongs to resize. In
   connect/pan/other tools, do not steal the tool's existing operation for
   rename. Only normal Select-tool click intent admits the new rename trigger.
5. A sidebar already open keeps its open state and selection-dependent
   Properties update without focus theft. Do not auto-close or force a closed
   sidebar open. Preserve chosen Markdown view/draft; Properties populate when
   that view is shown. Selection must not discard text/IME/history or change a
   user-selected panel mode as a side effect.
6. Preserve explicit Sidebar/View controls, F2 and context-menu Edit label,
   existing Enter/blur/Escape validation/commit/cancel policies, keyboard focus
   return and ownership/Present/modal guards. Read-only/Present selection
   cannot create an editing session. No new persisted fields or commands.
7. Guide/manual explain select first, then click the selected title to rename,
   plus keyboard/context alternatives and nonautomatic sidebar behavior.

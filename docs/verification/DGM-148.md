### Finding 1 — visible segment handle is mostly covered by the edge hit stroke

**Affected criteria:** AC2, AC3, AC6.
**Severity:** high-confidence interaction/usability defect; blocks acceptance.

- **Location:** `app/src/editor/EditorSurface.tsx:122` renders the transparent
  20-unit `.react-flow__edge-interaction` path and the segment-handle rectangles
  in the same edge group; handle styling is at
  `app/src/editor/editor.css:155-157`.
- **Reproduction:** open
  `app/test-results/dgm148/independent/run-20261102-retry7/fixture.json`
  using File > Open JSON, select the dashed `Main path`, and drag the center of
  its visible vertical segment handle. At the measured 25.5 × 25.5 px target
  (`desktop-light-before.png`), browser `elementFromPoint` at the center resolves
  to the edge path, not the handle. The retained failed probe in
  `run-20261102-retry6/report.json` records pointerdown targeted at
  `.react-flow__edge-interaction`, pointer capture false, subsequent movement
  over the pane, and an unchanged route.
- **Expected:** the visible, focusable handle receives pointer input across its
  target and starts a perpendicular route preview.
- **Actual:** the handle’s center and most sampled interior points resolve to
  the overlaid route stroke. A drag begun there does nothing. In the successful
  run, the same drag only worked from `box.left + 1`; the hit-grid sample found
  the handle at offsets 0, 1 and 24, while offsets 2, 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 hit
  the route path. This leaves only narrow exposed edge slivers of the displayed
  handle target. The drag succeeded from that sliver, but this does not make the
  rendered 25.5 px target reliably usable.
- **Measured successful adjustment from the edge sliver:** route x changed
  from 400 to 432.25 diagram units for a 44 screen-pixel horizontal pointer
  movement; endpoints stayed `(320,164)` and `(560,164)`. Preview preceded the
  one release commit. The path/style/arrow and unrelated document content were
  preserved.
- **Disposition:** do not accept pointer usability based on the successful
  edge-only retry or the DOM target’s nominal dimensions. The center-hit
  failure is retained, not repaired or discarded.
